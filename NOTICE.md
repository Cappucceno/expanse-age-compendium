The Portuguese terminology used by this system (names of abilities, focuses, talents, specializations, stunts, conditions, character creation options, weapons and armor) follows the Brazilian edition of *The Expanse RPG*:

- **Publisher:** Jambô Editora (https://jamboeditora.com.br) - 1st edition, May 2021, ISBN 978-65-88634-00-4
- **Translation:** Flávia Gasi and Gilvan Gouvêia
- **Edition:** Felipe Della Corte
- **Proofreading:** Glauco Lessa
- **Layout:** Alexandre Straube
- **Editor-in-chief:** Guilherme Dei Svaldi

All rights to the Brazilian edition and its text are reserved to Jambô Editora. This repository contains **only short names and labels** from that edition (the files in `lang/babele/pt-BR` and `lang/pt-BR.json`), listed for the purpose of using the same terminology in the game. It does not contain the translated descriptions or rules text, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jambô Editora. The Portuguese edition is available at the publisher's store.

Compendium names are translated only for the parts of the book covered by the Brazilian PDF used as reference (chapters 1 to 5 up to the general exploration and infiltration stunts). Everything else stays in English.
